About

My 'Memory Scrapbook' project is a digital scrapbook for experiences like concerts and adventures, built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There are no frameworks or libraries used; everything is hand-coded. The site organizes photos and videos into custom collections based on different memories with smooth sliding galleries, a custom video player, and a responsive design.
